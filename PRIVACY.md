# Privacy Policy — Commune Sky

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Commune Sky is an open-source weather app developed by Ali Haydar Sayar. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: **the developer collects nothing.**

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No advertising
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind
- No selling or sharing of personal data

## Location

If you grant the **approximate location** permission, the app uses your device's coarse location to show the weather where you are. Precise (GPS-level) location is never requested. If you don't grant the permission, the app shows the weather for a default city instead and works normally.

Your approximate coordinates are used in two ways:

1. **Weather forecast** — the coordinates are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) to retrieve the forecast. Open-Meteo states that it may keep server logs, which can include coordinates, for troubleshooting and deletes them after 90 days. See the [Open-Meteo privacy policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy).
2. **City name** — the coordinates are passed to Android's built-in Geocoder service, provided by your device's operating system, to look up the name of your city.

The developer never receives or stores your location.

## Data stored on your device

The most recent forecast is cached on your device so the app and its widgets can show it instantly and offline. This data is never sent to the developer (it may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on), and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
